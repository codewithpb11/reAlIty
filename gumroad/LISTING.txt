FULL DESCRIPTION (paste into the Description field)

Is that photo real, or AI?

**reAlIty** is a desktop app that analyzes images and videos and tells you whether they're likely AI-generated or human-made — with a confidence score for each.

Upload a file, hit Verify, and get a clear verdict in seconds.

---

**What you get**

✓ Full desktop app (Python source + pre-trained model included)
✓ Works offline — your files never leave your machine
✓ Supports images (.jpg, .png, .webp, .avif) and videos (.mp4, .avi, .mov)
✓ Video analysis samples multiple frames for a stable result
✓ Flags heavy text/graphic overlays that can skew detection
✓ Clean, minimal black-and-white interface
✓ MIT license — use personally or commercially

---

**Who it's for**

• Journalists and fact-checkers screening suspicious media
• Educators teaching media literacy
• Content creators verifying sources
• Anyone curious whether something online is real

---

**How it works**

reAlIty uses a fine-tuned SigLIP image classifier trained on thousands of AI and human images. For video, it samples frames and aggregates scores — no separate video model needed.

---

**Requirements**

• Windows 10/11 (Mac/Linux supported with manual setup)
• Python 3.10 or newer (free download)
• ~2 GB free disk space
• Internet for one-time dependency install

Setup takes about 5 minutes. A step-by-step QUICKSTART.txt is included in the download.

---

**Important — please read**

reAlIty is a **signal, not a verdict**. It's most accurate on clear-cut cases. Heavily edited, filtered, or ambiguous images can fool any detector. Do not rely on it alone for legal, journalistic, or high-stakes decisions. Results include a warning when graphic overlays (captions, arrows, redaction marks) are detected.
